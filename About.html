Meet The Human Behind The Camera

Hey there, I'm Whit

No age, no labels, no rigid boxes needed here—I'm just a human out here trying to figure out life, soaking up the good moments, and snapping photos of whatever makes me smile along the way.

I'm lucky to do this whole life thing alongside an awesome, endlessly supportive husband who always has my back. (And yes—the only reason he's smiling with me in this photo is because I got his official permission to post him!) We also have a kiddo together, though we keep their world tucked safely off the internet.

We recently made the move up from Florida and are settling into our new chapter as a proudly pet-friendly family. Wherever we land, my camera is usually close by.

I've always loved photography. More than anything, I just want people to feel my happiness through a camera lens—even if my own visual lenses are a little cracked at the moment and currently getting repaired so I can see the full brightness of it all. Until everything is crystal clear again, this site is how I share the joy I see right in front of me.

— Whit
