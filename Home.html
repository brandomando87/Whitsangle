Behind The Lens

Meet Whit — Chasing Real Emotion & twilight

I started Whit's Angle of Life Photography with one simple obsession: noticing the quiet angles everyone else walks right past—the way bridge lights ripple blue, gold, and crimson across dark river water, or the split second someone forgets the camera is even there.

Whether we are shooting on the Peoria riverfront after sundown, tucked into the Warehouse District, or out in golden Midwest fields, my priority is making you feel completely relaxed. I'll guide you with natural movement and easy prompts so your gallery looks bold, moody, and unmistakably you.

— Whit

Photographer & Visual Storyteller
